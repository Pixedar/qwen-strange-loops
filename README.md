# Strange Loops in Qwen

**What happens when a language model can act on a measurement of its own activity?**

Wiktor Tomasik · experiments begun 4–5 November 2026 · results through 8 November 2026

In one run, Qwen asked an instrument to link *explorer* with *discovery*. A later measurement returned *strangers*. Qwen acted on that word and used it to describe itself. In a different experiment, putting *volcano* into a readout without changing the activity inside Qwen was enough to make it choose the word for its next intervention.

[Read the full study](https://pixedar.github.io/projects/self-awareness/internal-mirror/) · [Read on GitHub](STUDY.md) · [Explore the measurements](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Pixedar/jspace-internal-mirror-evidence)

The study follows the loop from [the “strangers” run](https://pixedar.github.io/projects/self-awareness/internal-mirror/#strangers), through [what the readout changes](https://pixedar.github.io/projects/self-awareness/internal-mirror/#loop) and [what makes a forecast reach a choice](https://pixedar.github.io/projects/self-awareness/internal-mirror/#choice), to [tests with the words held fixed](https://pixedar.github.io/projects/self-awareness/internal-mirror/#activity). It closes by [comparing the route through words with the original external reader](https://pixedar.github.io/projects/self-awareness/internal-mirror/#compare).
